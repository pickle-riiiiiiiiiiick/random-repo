# Global Landscape of Toothpaste / Tooth-Cleaning Product Formats

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method note: web search worked, but WebFetch failed with DNS errors (ENOTFOUND) on most domains (grandviewresearch.com, fortunebusinessinsights.com, chemlinked.com, lebensmittelpraxis.de, pharmacytimes.com, kao.com). Most findings below therefore come from search-result excerpts, not full-page reads, so check them before quoting precisely. Market-research vendor figures disagree widely and should be presented as ranges.

## 1. Size and growth of the global toothpaste / oral-care market (2024–2026), and the split by format and region

### Takeaway
Global toothpaste is a mature, low-growth category (roughly 2.5–3.3% CAGR). Estimates of its size vary more than 2x depending on the vendor (about USD 18.5bn to 44bn). Colgate alone holds about 41% of global toothpaste value. Conventional tube paste and gel dominate. I found no credible public source that splits global value by physical format (paste vs gel vs powder vs tablet vs liquid vs foam). The best proxy is to size the niche formats separately: tablets are about USD 70–120m and foam is about USD 0.8bn (vendor claim). Both are well under 1–3% of the category.

### Cited Findings
**Global market size (conflicting vendor estimates)**
- Technavio: the global toothpaste market will grow by about USD 3.85bn between 2024 and 2029, at a 3.3% CAGR. — [Technavio](https://technavio.com/report/toothpaste-market-industry-analysis)
- WiseGuy Reports: the "Toothpaste Toothpowder Market" was valued at USD 44.4bn in 2025, at a 2.7% CAGR to 2035. It lists formulation segments as Gel, Paste, Powder, Liquid and Cream, and says gel and paste are "particularly prominent". The excerpt gave no segment values. — [WiseGuy Reports](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/toothpaste-toothpowder-market)
- SkyQuest: USD 18.51bn in 2024, at a 2.5% CAGR. This is a much lower base, which shows the definitional inconsistency between vendors. — [SkyQuest via GII](https://gii.tw/report/sky1646265-toothpaste-market-size-share-growth-analysis-by.html)
- Deep Market Insights: its Europe page shows a 2025 "gel" value of 5,824.56 against a regional total of 10,956.66, with units not stated (likely USD m). That would put gel at about 53% of Europe. The paste and powder cells were blank in the excerpt, so treat this as low confidence. — [Deep Market Insights – Europe](https://deepmarketinsights.com/vista/insights/toothpaste-market/europe); [Global page](https://deepmarketinsights.com/vista/insights/toothpaste-market/global)
- Global oral care is about USD 34.8bn in 2025, with toothpaste at a 56.62% share. This figure is attributed to Fortune Business Insights via a blog post. It is low confidence because the primary page could not be fetched. — [blog citing Fortune Business Insights](https://chetanap.digitalpress.blog/global-oral-care-market-industry-share-growth-and-analysis/)

**Market structure / leaders**
- Colgate-Palmolive reported a 40.9% year-to-date global toothpaste share in Q1 2025, down 0.5 points year on year, and a 31.9% share in manual toothbrushes. — [Colgate Q1 2025 10-Q (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/21665/000002166525000020/cl-20250331.htm); [Q1 2025 press release tables](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000021665/000002166525000019/q12025pressreleasetables.htm)
- Colgate's 2025 Annual Report claims "#1 Market Share in Toothpaste Worldwide" and record net sales of USD 20.4bn. — [Colgate 2025 Annual Report (SEC ARS)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/21665/000002166526000013/a2025annualreportfinal.pdf)
- Oral care was about 44% of Colgate's 2025 net sales. This comes from a secondary summary and was not verified against the filing. — [MarketScreener](https://au.marketscreener.com/news/colgate-palmolive-company-durable-global-compounder-ce7e5cdbdb8bfe27)

**Regional data points**
- China: IBISWorld puts the toothpaste and toothbrush manufacturing industry at USD 6.8bn in 2025, up 7.6% from 2024, and names Yunnan Baiyao among the largest companies. — [IBISWorld China](https://www.ibisworld.com/china/industry/toothpaste-and-toothbrush-manufacturing/319/)
- Japan: IMARC forecasts the Japanese toothpaste market to grow at a 1.20% CAGR (2024–2032), making it a mature market. — [IMARC Japan](https://imarcgroup.com/japan-toothpaste-market)
- Lion Corp. (Japan) claims the No.1 share in both toothpaste and toothbrushes in Japan ("2024 company research"). Its consolidated FY2025 sales were JPY 422.0bn, with business profit of JPY 30.7bn. — [Lion IR outline 2026](https://www.lion.co.jp/ja/ir/pdf/library/outline/t2026.pdf); [Lion IR outline 2025](https://lion.co.jp/ja/ir/pdf/library/outline/t202503_01.pdf)
- India: Colgate India's February 2025 analyst presentation states that "55% Rural Indians do not brush daily" and that rural per-capita toothpaste consumption is well below urban levels. — [Colgate-Palmolive India analyst meet, 21 Feb 2025](https://www.colgateinvestors.co.in/pdf/analyst-meet-presentation-21022025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Given the 2.4x spread between SkyQuest (USD 18.5bn) and WiseGuy (USD 44.4bn, which includes powder), the report should cite a range (about USD 20–45bn depending on scope) and lean on the more defensible Colgate share data (about 41%) as the anchor for market structure.
- All non-paste formats summed are almost certainly in the low single-digit percent of global dentifrice value. Tablets at about USD 0.07–0.12bn are around 0.2–0.5% of a USD 20–45bn market. Even the vendor-claimed foam figure (USD 0.8bn) would be about 2–4%.
- Growth is concentrated in emerging markets: India and rural brushing frequency, and China at +7.6% in 2025 per IBISWorld. Japan and Western markets are flat to low growth, where value growth comes from premiumisation (whitening, sensitivity, enzyme and hydroxyapatite actives) rather than volume.

### Gaps
- No primary or credible public split of global toothpaste value by physical format (paste/gel/powder/liquid/foam/tablet). Euromonitor/Mintel/NielsenIQ hold this behind paywalls.
- The Grand View Research and Fortune Business Insights pages could not be fetched (DNS failure), so their 2024–2026 sizes, regional shares (Asia Pacific is commonly cited as the largest region, but this is unverified here) and channel splits are missing.
- No verified full-year 2025 Colgate global toothpaste share. Only the Q1 2025 YTD figure (40.9%) was found.

## 2. Non-paste formats with meaningful market traction, and where

### Takeaway
The non-paste formats with real mass-market traction are regional: tooth powder and Ayurvedic/herbal pastes in India, herbal/TCM-positioned pastes in China (Yunnan Baiyao at about 25% share), liquid and foam dentifrices plus enzyme and hydroxyapatite pastes in Japan, and miswak/chewing sticks in the Middle East, South Asia and Africa. Toothpaste tablets have a visible but small and partly faltering niche in German-speaking Europe. Sales there rose to about EUR 3.5m for the pioneer Denttabs by 2020, then softened as the zero-waste trend faded.

### Cited Findings
**India – tooth powder, herbal/Ayurvedic paste, miswak-branded paste**
- The only tooth-powder share figure found is old: in 2008 Colgate held 46% of India's tooth powder market. It should not be treated as current. — [Business Standard (2008)](https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/reasons-to-smile-108102001054_1.html)
- Colgate's share of Indian toothpaste peaked at about 57% in FY15 and fell to about 48% by 2020 as herbal/Ayurvedic competitors (Patanjali, Dabur) gained. — [HDFC Securities initiating coverage, Dec 2021](https://www.hdfcsec.com/hsl.research.pdf/Colgate-Palmolive%20(India)%20Ltd%20-Initiating%20Coverage-HSLRR131221-.pdf); [Business Standard 2017](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/new-launches-rural-uptick-can-revive-colgate-s-fortunes-117121100264_1.html)
- 2020 data (via Dental Tribune 2022): Dabur held 13.4% of Indian toothpaste by value and Patanjali 9.2%. Together they held about 75% of the "natural" sub-segment. — [Zappchai Substack, citing Dental Tribune](https://zappchai.substack.com/p/toothpaste-battles)
- Dabur's toothpaste business grew 14.3% year on year in Q2 FY26 (Jul–Sep 2025), driven by Dabur Red paste and the premium Meswak brand (a miswak/salvadora-based paste). — [Business Standard, Oct 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/dabur-india-q2-pat-jumps-6-yoy-to-rs-453-cr-125103100549_1.html)
- Dabur has become the second-largest oral care player in Indian modern trade. — [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/news/dabur-strengthens-position-indias-retail-oral-care-market)
- Historically, at least 250 million Indians did not use toothpaste at all, and rural marketers used village campaigns (Colgate across about 400 villages in Orissa). This explains the persistence of powders, datun/twigs and home remedies. The article is older (date not given in the excerpt). — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/world/fight-for-indian-hearts-and-smiles-1.211638)

**China – herbal/TCM-positioned paste (Yunnan Baiyao)**
- Yunnan Baiyao's domestic toothpaste share rose from 22.2% (2020) to 25% (H1 2023). Nielsen data cited by Gelonghui put it at 24.6% for 2023. It was also the No.1 online toothpaste brand, with 11.2% of retail sales in Jan–Aug 2023. — [ChemLinked report (search excerpt)](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/report/keeping-pace-with-the-changing-landscape-and-trends-in-chinas-toothpaste-market); [Moomoo 000538.SZ](https://moomoo.com/stock/000538-SZ)
- Yunnan Baiyao has since launched new lines such as "High Altitude Liulan" (a high-altitude salt-themed line) and "Jing Shuang Peppermint". — [ChemLinked (search excerpt)](https://market.chemlinked.com/report/keeping-pace-with-the-changing-landscape-and-trends-in-chinas-toothpaste-market)

**Korea – salt and premium functional pastes**
- LG Household & Health Care's premium oral-care brands named in recent trade profiles include Himalaya Pink Salt (a salt toothpaste), Propolithera and Dr. Groot. Bamboo Salt (Jukyeom) was not named in the excerpt. — [Happi – LG H&H top company profile 2026](https://www.happi.com/top-company-profile/lg-household-healthcare/?cyear=2026)

**Japan – liquid dentifrice, foam, enzyme pastes**
- Kao sells a liquid toothpaste (Clear Clean liquid). Usage: take about 15 ml (half a cap) into the mouth, swish for 20 seconds, then brush. No rinse is needed afterward. It is medicated (a quasi-drug), whitening-positioned, contains alcohol and must not be swallowed. Kao's site groups it under "Liquid toothpaste・Foaming toothpaste", which confirms that both formats exist as a mainstream category in Japan. — [Kao product page (search excerpt)](https://www.kao.com/jp/en/products/clearclean/4901301203250/)
- Lion's premium "Clinica PRO All-in-one" paste (95 g, JPY 536) is positioned on a plaque-decomposing enzyme (dextranase), claimed as "Japan's only" such active ingredient, plus high-concentration fluoride. Enzyme pastes are a Japanese differentiator that is rare in Western markets. — [Sundrug Online listing](https://sundrug-online.com/en/products/4903301339038); [W Cosmetics AU](https://www.wcosmetics.com.au/products/lion-clinica-toothpaste-fresh-mint-130g)

**Germany / DACH – toothpaste tablets**
- Denttabs (Berlin) grew from about EUR 200k in annual sales (2015) to about EUR 3.5m by the start of the pandemic. A 2024 trade article reports that demand has since declined as the zero-packaging (Unverpackt) trend lost momentum as a driver. — [Lebensmittel Praxis, "Bleiben Zahnputztabletten ein Nischenprodukt?" (search excerpt)](https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/drogerieartikel/40864-plastikfreie-mundhygiene-bleiben-zahnputztabletten-ein-nischenprodukt.html)
- The breakthrough came around 2019, when dm (drugstore chain) listed Denttabs. dm's private label Dontodent tablets are reportedly made by Denttabs. dm prices Denttabs at EUR 5.95 per 125 tablets, with and without fluoride (about EUR 0.048 per brush). — [VBKI 2023](https://vbki.de/en/2023/02/08/tablette-statt-tube/); [dm.de Denttabs](https://www.dm.de/marken/denttabs-48140)

**Middle East / South Asia / Africa – miswak (Salvadora persica) chewing sticks**
- WHO recommended and encouraged miswak as an oral-hygiene tool, reportedly in 1986 or 1987 (sources disagree), mainly on grounds of tradition, availability and low cost. — [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04561960](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04561960); [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3545237); [Wikipedia – Siwak](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siwak)
- Efficacy evidence: small trials show miswak can equal a toothbrush for plaque and gingival outcomes (Malik et al. 2014), or beat it on *S. mutans* reduction (2004, n=40). Toothbrush plus miswak gave the best outcomes (2012, n=30). A Sudanese population study (n=203) found better periodontal status among miswak users. The 2000 international consensus called for more research. — [Dimensions of Dental Hygiene](https://dimensionsofdentalhygiene.com/using-miswak-alternative-oral-hygiene-method/); [PAFMJ](https://www.pafmj.org/PAFMJ/article/download/7295/3577); [Academia.edu](https://academia.edu/18366861)

### Inferences
- In India the "format" competition is less paste vs powder and more modern fluoride paste vs naturals/Ayurvedic positioning. Naturals (Dabur Red, Meswak, Patanjali Dant Kanti) took share from Colgate between 2015 and 2020. Powder (Dabur Red Tooth Powder, Colgate Tooth Powder, dant manjan) persists mainly in rural, low-income and older consumers, but I could not find current share data.
- Yunnan Baiyao's roughly 25% share means China's leading toothpaste is a TCM-positioned functional paste (gum bleeding). Western multinationals there compete against a "medicinal herbal" format logic with no Western equivalent.
- In Germany, tablets' strong retail visibility (dm private label) has not translated into large volumes. EUR 3.5m for the category pioneer, against a German toothpaste market in the hundreds of millions of euros, means the format is well under 1%.

### Gaps
- No current (2023–2026) tooth-powder market size or share for India. Dabur and Colgate annual reports do not break this out in the excerpts found.
- No sales or share data found for Korean salt toothpaste (LG H&H Bamboo Salt/Jukyeom, Himalaya Pink Salt) or Chinese salt toothpastes (e.g., Yunnan Baiyao salt lines, Lion China "Zhuyan" bamboo salt). Unverified background: bamboo salt paste is widely sold in Korea and China, but this was not confirmed with a source in this session.
- No data found on the share of liquid dentifrice in Japan. Lion's own liquid lines (e.g., "Clinica Ad Vantage"/"Systema" liquid dentifrice) were not confirmed in search results.
- No data on tablets' traction in the Nordics specifically.
- Miswak/datun usage prevalence and commercial market size (Middle East, Pakistan, sub-Saharan Africa) were not found.

## 3. Niche-but-growing formats and their growth rates

### Takeaway
Vendor estimates put toothpaste tablets at about USD 67–120m with roughly 6–10% CAGR, and foam toothpaste at about USD 0.8bn with a 7.2% CAGR (low-confidence vendor). Both outgrow the overall category (about 2.5–3.3%). Germany shows that tablet growth can stall once the eco/zero-waste fashion cools. Nano-hydroxyapatite pastes (a Japanese invention) are a fast-rising "fluoride-alternative" active in the US, but I found no market size.

### Cited Findings
**Toothpaste tablets / chewable tablets ("bits")**
- IMARC: USD 67.3m in 2023, rising to USD 115.6m by 2032, at a 6.19% CAGR (2024–2032). — [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/toothpaste-tablet-market)
- Global Market Insights: USD 86.9m in 2024, growing at a 7.5% CAGR (2025–2034) to about USD 177.9m by 2034. Drivers are eco-friendly/plastic-free packaging and travel convenience. — [GMI](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/toothpaste-tablets-market)
- Allied Market Research: USD 45.6m in 2020, rising to USD 90.3m by 2030 at a 7.3% CAGR. — [Allied](https://alliedmarketresearch.com/press-release/toothpaste-tablet-market.html)
- Future Market Insights: more than 6.1% CAGR for 2021–2031. — [Dentistry Today](https://dentistrytoday.com/?p=63650)
- An outlier vendor estimate is 10.03% CAGR, from USD 120.2m (2024) to USD 234.68m (2031). Its provenance is low quality. — [foriio summary](https://foriio.com/works/1726982)
- Efficacy: an RCT in 127 children aged 6–12 found a statistically significant difference in plaque scores between chewable tablets (950 ppm fluoride) and conventional toothpaste after 4 weeks. It measured plaque, not caries. — [Saudi Journal of Oral Sciences 2022 (DOAJ)](https://doaj.org/article/06659b6ff6a74ed284422ab158deae16)
- A January 2026 trade article reports that tablets are "gaining popularity" in US pharmacy and cites a 2025 Dent J study on dentifrice tablets and caries. The findings were not retrievable. — [Pharmacy Times, 6 Jan 2026](https://pharmacytimes.com/view/toothpaste-tablets-are-gaining-popularity-here-s-where-pharmacists-fit-in)

**Foam / mousse toothpaste**
- Global foam toothpaste is put at USD 0.8bn (2025), at a 7.2% CAGR to 2034. The same vendor claims foam is 22–25% of toothpaste sales in Japan and Korea, against a 15–18% global average. **This is very likely unreliable.** It conflicts with the near-absence of foam in Western retail and has no primary data behind it. — [Marketintelo (vendor)](https://marketintelo.com/report/foam-toothpaste-market)
- Lion developed an easy-rinse foam toothpaste for astronauts. JAXA selected it for the ISS (2022). Lion said it would look at terrestrial uses where water is scarce. — [Cosmetics Design Asia, Jan 2022](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-asia.com/Article/2022/01/17/Space-toothpaste-Japan-s-Lion-develops-easy-rinse-foam-toothpaste-for-astronauts)

**Nano-hydroxyapatite (nHAp) paste – active-ingredient innovation from Japan**
- The concept traces to NASA research (around 1970). Sangi Co. (Japan) bought the rights and launched the "world's first enamel restoration toothpaste" (Apagard) in 1980. nHAp is now regarded as a gold-standard oral ingredient in Japan. — [Today's RDH](https://www.todaysrdh.com/?p=10226); [RDH Magazine](https://www.rdhmag.com/patient-care/article/14276225/nano-hydroxyapatite-in-dental-products-better-than-fluoride)
- Efficacy: in situ, nHAp remineralised initial caries about as well as a 500 ppm fluoride paste. Another comparison found no significant difference from fluoride. A clinician view is that it is a good alternative or adjunct but not an ideal full replacement for fluoride. US FDA approval status is described as not yet granted (trade press), even though products are sold at US retail. — [RDH Magazine](https://www.rdhmag.com/patient-care/article/14276225/nano-hydroxyapatite-in-dental-products-better-than-fluoride)

### Inferences
- Tablets and foam both grow at about 2–3x the category rate, but from tiny bases. Tablets are a "sustainability/travel" proposition. Foam is a sensory and water-saving proposition, with relevance for kids, the elderly and care homes.
- The German experience (pioneer sales falling after about 2020) is a cautionary signal for Western Europe: eco-driven formats need a functional or sensory benefit beyond packaging to sustain growth.
- Fluoride compatibility is a key regulatory and efficacy question for tablets. Fluoride variants exist (e.g., dm Denttabs with fluoride; trial tablets at 950 ppm), but many "natural" tablets are fluoride-free or use nHAp instead.

### Gaps
- No credible growth data found for: tooth soaps, solid toothpaste bars/sticks, toothpaste strips/films/dissolvable sheets, single-dose stick-packs/sachets, pre-pasted disposable brushes (hotel/travel/airline amenity), pump dispensers, toothpaste-mouthwash 2-in-1s, drops/concentrates, or refill systems. These formats were not covered by sources retrievable in this session.
- No nHAp toothpaste market size (US/EU) was found.
- The status of ADA Seal of Acceptance for tablets was not confirmed.

## 4. Asian formats (Japan, Korea, China) not yet mainstream in Western Europe / North America

### Takeaway
The clearest "Asia-first" formats are liquid toothpaste used as a swish-then-brush step, foaming/mousse dentifrices, enzyme pastes (dextranase), nano-hydroxyapatite pastes, salt and bamboo-salt pastes, and TCM-positioned medicinal pastes. All are established in at least one major Asian market and still marginal in the West. nHAp is the one already crossing into US/EU niche retail.

### Cited Findings
- Japan liquid toothpaste: Kao's medicated liquid toothpaste is used as a 15 ml swish for 20 s, then brushing, with no rinse required. Kao markets it as a "Liquid toothpaste・Foaming toothpaste" category. — [Kao](https://www.kao.com/jp/en/products/clearclean/4901301203250/)
- Japan foam: Lion's easy-rinse foam toothpaste for astronauts (ISS, 2022) points to water-scarce and care-setting uses. — [Cosmetics Design Asia](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-asia.com/Article/2022/01/17/Space-toothpaste-Japan-s-Lion-develops-easy-rinse-foam-toothpaste-for-astronauts)
- Japan enzyme paste: Lion's Clinica PRO uses a plaque-decomposing enzyme (dextranase) with high-concentration fluoride. — [Sundrug Online](https://sundrug-online.com/en/products/4903301339038); [W Cosmetics AU](https://www.wcosmetics.com.au/products/lion-clinica-toothpaste-fresh-mint-130g)
- Japan nHAp: Sangi's Apagard has been sold since 1980 and is described as a Japanese market leader. It is now sold at US retail (e.g., Weee!). — [Today's RDH](https://www.todaysrdh.com/?p=10226); [Weee! listing](https://www.sayweee.com/en/product/apagard-premio-hydroxyapatite-toothpaste/3000816)
- Korea salt paste: LG H&H's Himalaya Pink Salt is a premium oral-care line. — [Happi 2026](https://www.happi.com/top-company-profile/lg-household-healthcare/?cyear=2026)
- China TCM/medicinal paste: Yunnan Baiyao holds about 25% domestic share (H1 2023) and has altitude- and salt-themed line extensions. — [ChemLinked](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/report/keeping-pace-with-the-changing-landscape-and-trends-in-chinas-toothpaste-market)
- Japan's market is mature (1.2% CAGR), so innovation there runs through format and active-ingredient premiumisation rather than volume. — [IMARC Japan](https://imarcgroup.com/japan-toothpaste-market)

### Inferences
- Relevance to Western Europe / North America:
  - **Liquid toothpaste (swish-then-brush)** is a format bridge between mouthwash and paste. It could be a 2-in-1 white space in the West, but alcohol content and "do not swallow" warnings limit its use for children.
  - **Foam** fits kids, elderly or assisted care, and travel.
  - **Enzyme and nHAp actives** align with Western "fluoride-free"/"natural" demand. nHAp is already the leading candidate in the US.
  - **Salt and bamboo salt** pastes have a heritage and gum-health story, but low Western awareness.
- None of these Asian formats has displaced tube paste in its home market either. They coexist as premium or segment-specific options.

### Gaps
- No market-share data for liquid or foam toothpaste in Japan from a primary source (e.g., INTAGE retail panel, Lion/Kao/Sunstar IR).
- No sourced information on Korean innovations beyond salt (e.g., Korean pump/"tube-free" formats, propolis pastes, or Korean tablet brands).
- No sourced data on Chinese emerging formats (probiotic paste, mousse "lazy" toothpaste, e-commerce-driven tablets).
- Format descriptions requested but not verified with sources in this session, to be treated as background only: tooth soap (origin US/UK artisanal); solid toothpaste bars/sticks (e.g., Lush, and Unilever's Signal/Prodent tube-free experiments, unverified); toothpaste strips/films; single-dose sachets and stick-packs (common in emerging-market sachet economies); pre-pasted disposable brushes (hotel/airline amenities, Asia); pump dispensers (popular in Germany historically, unverified); refill systems; dental chewing gum (xylitol gum, strongly marketed in Japan and Finland, unverified); oral-care drops/concentrates. Price positioning, efficacy and waste comparisons for these were not sourced in this session.
