# Follow-up: what toothpaste startups have done

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method: about 16 web searches run directly (no page fetches). Figures come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Revenue, funding and traction numbers are mostly company-reported or from aggregators and should be treated as indicative. This file adds to section 2 of competitive_landscape.md.

## Eco tablets and refill systems

- **Smyle (Netherlands, founded 2020; Almar Fernhout, Dennis Kamst, Roger Nefkens).** Plastic-free vegan toothpaste tablets. Crowdfunded throughout: €200k via CrowdAboutNow ([De Betere Wereld](https://www.debeterewereld.nl/lichaam-geest/oprichters-smyle-halen-twee-ton-op-met-duurzame-tandpasta-tabletten-en-starten-met-biologische-certificering-en-nieuwe-smaken/)); €1,156,660 from 1,441 people via Broccoli in Nov 2022 ([Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/smyle-bags-more-than-1m/)); a later "final public sharefunding" round. Company says it grew more than 50% in 2024 to nearly €3m revenue ([Smyle](https://wesmyle.com/pages/sharefund)).
- **Georganics (UK, Sussex; founded ~2014–2016, sources differ).** Tablets, powders, mouthwash tablets, COSMOS Natural; own small factory; also distributes Denttabs. Republic crowdfund funded 5 Aug 2024: £377,828 from 195 investors at £2.8m pre-money; claims 1,000+ points of sale and 15 countries ([Republic](https://europe.republic.com/georganics)).
- **Huppy (US).** Tablets in a refillable tin with compostable refills, 1% for the Planet member; described as still growing in an Amazon seller profile dated Aug 2026 ([Amazon](https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/a-backpacking-trip-inspired-this-former-banker-to-reinvent-toothpaste-without-plastic)).
- **Unpaste (US).** Described as the first US toothpaste tablet available with fluoride, also sold fluoride-free ([Garage Grown Gear](https://garagegrowngear.com/blogs/gear-gab/huppy-unpaste-best-toothpaste-tabs)). No closure found for Huppy, Unpaste or Hello tablets.
- **Change Toothpaste (Canada).** Pea-size bite-and-brush tablets ([Springwise](https://springwise.com/circular-economy/canadian-startup-creates-zero-waste-toothpaste)).
- **900.care (France, Paris).** Water-free refill subscription for bathroom products (shower gel, deodorant, hand foam, toothpaste tablets); reusable bottle on first order. Raised €10m (Jun 2021; 360 Capital, White Star, Founders Future) and €21m (Mar 2024, led by Lombard Odier IM). Revenue almost tripled to over €10m in 2023; about 235,000 active subscriptions; target €100m revenue within three years ([Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2024/03/26/900-care-leve-21-millions-deuros-pour-atteindre-100-millions-deuros-de-revenus-dici-trois-ans/); [Premium Beauty News](https://www.premiumbeautynews.com/en/900-care-raises-21-million-euros,23624)). Added dissolvable toothpaste pastilles at about €2.49 (listed summer 2025) ([LSA](https://www.lsa-conso.fr/marques-responsables-900-care-etoffe-sa-gamme-avec-un-dentifrice-a-dissoudre,463590)). No 2025–2026 revenue update found.
- **Bite (US, founded 2017, Marina del Rey).** Now sells toothpaste bits, mouthwash bits and bamboo brushes, with fluoride and fluoride-free options ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/bite-toothpaste-bits-vs-quip1)). No credible 2025–2026 acquisition or funding news; acquisition claims on "net worth" sites conflict and are unreliable.

## Benefit-led ingredient brands

- **Davids (US).** Natural paste; 2018 launch in all ~450 Whole Foods, then Sprouts, Gelson's, Erewhon; first $1m sales year in 2019; moved to a 15,000 sq ft Menifee, CA facility in 2023 ([Dentistry Today](https://www.dentistrytoday.com/?p=126705)). Launched nano-HAp "Hydroxi" in 2022 ([GCI](https://www.gcimagazine.com/brands-products/oral-care/news/21863630/davids-launches-nano-hydroxyapatite-toothpaste)). Claims SPINS dollar-growth leadership in natural-channel oral care ([Expo West](https://attend.expowest.com/event/natural-products-expo-west-2026/exhibitor/RXhoaWJpdG9yXzIzNTY3NDY=)). No funding data found.
- **Theodent (US, New Orleans; launched 4 Jan 2012).** Fluoride alternative from cocoa-derived theobromine ("Rennou"), from LSU research by Dr Tetsuo Nakamoto; company-linked trials claim superiority to fluoride ([Cosmetics Design](https://cosmeticsdesign.com/Article/2012/01/18/Toothpaste-launch-replaces-fluoride-with-chocolate); [Silicon Bayou](https://siliconbayounews.com/2013/02/15/bioscience-toothpaste-company-theodent-is-revolutionizing-oral-care/)). Funding undisclosed. Remains niche.
- **Biomin (UK, Queen Mary University of London spin-out).** Bioactive-glass toothpaste; £2m ($2.6m) round backed by Guangdong Kanwan Cosmetics; licenses technology (Dr Collins in the US, Group Pharmaceuticals, Abbott); sells in Europe, China, India, Australia ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/biomin-polishes-2.6m-round); [QMUL](https://www.sems.qmul.ac.uk/news/5018)).

## Beauty, design and device systems

- **Cocolab (formerly Cocofloss, US; dentist co-founder).** Rebranded to cover a broader range ([Beauty Independent](https://www.beautyindependent.com/cult-favorite-cocofloss-rebrands-cocolab-growing-oral-care-repertoire/)). Sales up 5x in five years; Inc. 5000 2025 (177% three-year growth); in about two-thirds of CVS stores; Target nationwide from Jan 2026; about 8,000 dentists in its professional channel; marketing budget up 200%+ (company/Amazon case study) ([Amazon](https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/the-sisters-turning-their-infectious-love-of-oral-hygiene-and-design-into-a-battle-against-gum-disease)). No funding round found.
- **Happybrush (Germany, Munich).** Electric brushes plus toothpaste, floss, mouthwash. €6m+ revenue in 2017 (25x on 2016); 2021 round of more than €4m led by BayBG and Haniel, profitable at the time ([Startbase](https://www.startbase.com/news/happybrush-sammelt-rund-vier-millionen-euro-ein/)); 2026 Sevenventures (ProSiebenSat.1) mid-single-digit-million investment with Manuel Neuer as partner; about 25m products sold ([Munich Startup](https://www.munich-startup.de/en/news/happybrush-signs-manuel-neuer-as-strategic-partner)).
- **SURI (UK).** Sustainable electric toothbrush by ex-P&G founders; £2m seed (2022), £6m Series A (2024, JamJar and V3 Ventures); sales over £10m in 2023 ([City AM](https://www.cityam.com/?p=2134644)). No toothpaste confirmed.
- **Moon (US, 2019; Beach House Group, Kendall Jenner).** Beat its Ulta sales plan by 35%; whitening pen over $1m at Ulta; 8,000+ doors within six months; later Boots UK ([WWD via Najafi](https://najafi.com/kendall-jenner-backed-brand-wants-to-make-your-mouth-beautiful); [Cosmetics Business](https://www.cosmeticsbusiness.com/oral-care-brand-moon-prepares-to-land-in-uk-market-at-boots--162822)). No recent sales data.
- **Marvis (Italy).** Old brand (registered 1958 per one source) revived by Ludovico Martelli from 1996/97 as a design-led luxury paste; made in Italy; US via C.O. Bigelow ([Fashion Week Daily](https://fashionweekdaily.com/beauty-made-in-italy-marvis/)). Not a Colgate brand; no revenue found.
- **Hello (US).** Acquired by Colgate (announced Jan 2020) from Tenth Avenue Holdings; founder Craig Dubitsky; brand skewed millennial ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200123005777/en/Colgate-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Hello-Oral-Care-Brand)).

## Strips and films
- No dissolvable toothpaste film startup found. Dissolving strips in the West are whitening products (Supersmile Superstrips, Listerine Quick Dissolving Strips) ([Drug Store News](https://drugstorenews.com/news/whats-hot-dissolving-strips-spark-home-whitening-segment)).

## Inferences
- Tablet brands finance themselves through crowdfunding and bootstrapping; the largest found (Smyle, ~€3m revenue) is still small. Big money went to systems and brands (quip, 900.care, Hismile, Spotlight), not to a tablet.
- 900.care is the strongest evidence that water-free refills can scale by subscription, and it already sells toothpaste pastilles in France: a direct competitor for a France launch.
- The professional channel is a recurring growth lever (Spotlight crowdfunding from Invisalign providers, Cocolab's 8,000 dentists, Biomin licensing).
- Brands pivot from eco to benefit when growth stalls (Bite adding nHAp and fluoride, Unpaste and Denttabs offering fluoride).

## Gaps
- No verified failures or shutdowns of toothpaste startups.
- No funding data for Davids, Cocolab, Huppy, Unpaste, Ben & Anna (glass-jar paste, Germany) or Marvis.
- No 2025–2026 revenue for 900.care, Bite or Moon.
