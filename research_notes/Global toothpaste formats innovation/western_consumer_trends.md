# Western Consumer Trends and Demand Signals in Toothpaste (Western Europe + North America, 2023–2026)

Research note: 18 tool calls. Every WebFetch call failed with DNS errors (ENOTFOUND for carequest.org, omya.com, beautymatter.com and cnn.com), so the findings below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Market-size figures from syndicated "market research" publishers (IMARC, Market Data Forecast, Grand View, etc.) disagree widely, use different definitions, and are low-reliability. Company filings (Haleon, Colgate) are the most reliable data here.

## 1. Market size, growth and premium share (US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, NL, Nordics)

### Takeaway
Toothpaste is a slow-growing, price-led category in the West, at roughly 3–4% a year by value. The US is about USD 5.5bn, the UK about USD 0.9bn and Germany about EUR 2bn for all oral care. Growth is coming from premium "therapeutic" and science-led sub-brands (Sensodyne Clinical, parodontax, elmex, Optic White Pro) rather than from volume. Haleon's oral health business grew organically by high single digits while Colgate's segment volume fell.

### Cited Findings
- US toothpaste market: about USD 5.49bn in 2025, with a forecast CAGR of 3.05% to 2034. Supermarkets and hypermarkets hold 41% of US toothpaste sales. — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/united-states-toothpaste-market)
- US toothpaste market: USD 5.47bn in 2025, with a 3.18% CAGR to 2034 (this broadly agrees with IMARC). — [Market Data Forecast](https://www.marketdataforecast.com/market-reports/united-states-toothpaste-market)
- Global toothpaste market estimates for 2025 range from USD 22.3bn (3.26% CAGR) through USD 23.5bn (4.8% CAGR) to USD 27.4bn. — [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/toothpaste); [Market Research Future](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/toothpaste-market-7221); [Market.us](https://www.news.market.us/toothpaste-market-news/)
- UK toothpaste market: about USD 909m in 2025, with a 4.1–4.2% CAGR to 2034/35. Sensitivity products make up an estimated 44.8% of UK toothpaste value in 2025. — [Market Data Forecast UK](https://www.marketdataforecast.com/market-reports/united-kingdom-toothpaste-market); [MRFR UK](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/uk-toothpaste-market-46324)

Fortune Business Insights puts Germany's oral care market at about USD 2.02bn in 2025. dm and Rossmann are the #1 and #2 German drugstore chains by revenue. — [Statista/IKW](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/166766/umfrage/umsatz-mit-zahn-und-mundpflegemitteln-seit-2007/); [Fortune BI](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/oral-care-market-103533); [Statista drugstores](https://www.statista.com/statistics/505579/revenue-of-drugstore-chains-in-germany/)

### Inferences
- Category value growth of about 3–4% a year is mostly price and mix. The two largest players show premium therapeutic lines (sensitivity plus whitening, gum health, enamel) outgrowing the category: Haleon Oral Health grew 7–9% organic against Colgate's segment at about 1% with negative volume. A new format would probably capture value faster if it carries a clinical or therapeutic claim than if it relies on sustainability alone.
- Sensitivity is the largest UK sub-segment (about 45%), and Sensodyne's Clinical White success points to "sensitivity + whitening" combination benefits as the mainstream premium sweet spot.
- Premium-priced, clinically positioned lines are working with younger consumers, according to Haleon's penetration data.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2024–2026 toothpaste market sizes were found for Canada, France, the Netherlands or the Nordics. I found no Circana, NielsenIQ, Kantar or Euromonitor figures for any country.
- No quantified premium share of toothpaste sales was found for any country. Sources say premium is "gaining" but give no number.

## 2. Sustainability and zero-waste: tube waste, recyclable tubes, tablets/bars

### Takeaway
Tube waste is a large, well-publicised problem: about 20bn tubes a year globally and 1–1.5bn in the US, according to Colgate. Big brands responded by making tubes recyclable (single-material HDPE) rather than changing format. Toothpaste tablets remain a small niche. Credible data on their sales is very thin, and the identified barriers are habit, taste and texture, price, perceived efficacy (often fluoride-free), and online-only distribution.

### Cited Findings
- Colgate says about 20bn toothpaste tubes a year go to landfill globally. — [Colgate press release](https://www.colgatepalmolive.com/en-us/news/recyclable-toothpaste-tube-recycle-me-packaging-us); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-03-03/can-toothpaste-tubes-be-recycled-colgate-says-its-tubes-are-now-recyclable)
- US estimates: about 1bn tubes a year, or 1.5bn according to Colgate's consumer page. Slashdot, summarising Bloomberg, cites "9 billion" Colgate tubes a year (basis unverified). — [Colgate Sustainable Habits](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/mission/environmental-impact/habits); [Slashdot](https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/12/09/1724221/colgates-9-billion-toothpaste-tubes-defy-effort-to-recycle-them)
- Colgate redesigned its tube from mixed plastics and aluminium into single-material HDPE after more than 5 years of development. It targeted 100% recyclable tubes in the US by 2023 and globally by 2025, a transition that began in 2019. — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90713412/after-149-years-colgates-toothpaste-tubes-are-finally-recyclable); [Colgate Sustainable Products](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/mission/environmental-impact/products)
- "Recyclable is not recycled": Bloomberg reported that many US sorting centres don't accept the tubes and that How2Recycle didn't classify them as recyclable at the time. Colgate itself warns acceptance may be limited during the transition. — [Bloomberg via Slashdot](https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/12/09/1724221/colgates-9-billion-toothpaste-tubes-defy-effort-to-recycle-them); [Colgate](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/mission/environmental-impact/habits)
- Toothpaste tablet market estimates are small and inconsistent. One estimate is about USD 152m globally by 2026 (2022 forecast). Grand View estimates Europe reaches about USD 41.8m by 2030, is the fastest-growing region, and held 34.4% of global tablet revenue in 2023, with Germany at 25.3% of Europe in 2023. Other reports say North America is the largest market, and one outlier claims USD 62.97bn globally, which is not credible. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/10/19/2537681/0/en/Toothpaste-Tablet-Market-All-Set-to-Exceed-US-152-3-Mn-in-Revenue-by-2026-End.html); [Grand View Europe](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/toothpaste-tablet-market/europe); [Grand View global](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/toothpaste-tablet-market-report)
- Barriers to tablet adoption: consumers are used to paste, and chewing a tablet feels unfamiliar. Adoption is limited by texture, taste and price sensitivity. Distribution skews heavily to online and specialty eco stores. A cited (unverified) European Consumer Organisation figure says 33% of users found availability limited in rural areas. — [IJSRT review](https://www.ijsrtjournal.com/article/a-comprehensive-review-on-toothpaste-tablets-formulation-benefits-and-future-prospects); [Future Market Insights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/toothpaste-tablets-market); [Fortune BI](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/toothpaste-tablets-market-112276)
- Bite (the US tablet leader) is modest in scale. Third-party revenue estimates range from about USD 4–6m to USD 8.8m a year (low reliability). Sales run mainly through a DTC subscription and Amazon (4.2-star average). Travel and leak-free use is a cited use case, and the brand has extended into mouthwash bits, whitening gel, floss and brushes. — [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Bite_Toothpaste_Bits); [CNBC 2021](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/06/03/how-a-founder-turned-6000-into-a-multimillion-dollar-toothpaste-business.html); [Shark Tank Success](https://sharktanksuccess.com/bite-toothpaste-update-after-shark-tank/)

### Inferences
- Even the most optimistic tablet estimates (tens to low hundreds of USD millions globally) are under 1% of a USD 22–27bn toothpaste market. Stated eco-intent has not turned into mass adoption. Tablets succeed as a DTC subscription, travel and eco-niche product, not as a mainstream replacement.
- Incumbents' "recyclable tube" move removes some of the sustainability differentiation a new format could claim. A new format probably needs a functional or sensory benefit (dosing, mess-free, travel, efficacy parity with fluoride) in addition to waste reduction.

### Gaps
- No credible consumer willingness-to-pay data for sustainable toothpaste was found (for example, a % premium a Mintel or Kantar survey would accept).
- No verified retail scanner sales for tablets or bars in the US, UK or Germany (including dm and Rossmann private-label tablets).
- No data on actual recycling rates achieved for HDPE tubes in 2024–2026.

## 3. Natural, clean-label and fluoride-free; US fluoride politics; hydroxyapatite boom

### Takeaway
The fluoride-free and hydroxyapatite (nano-HAp) segment is the clearest fast-growing demand signal in US toothpaste. NielsenIQ data reported by Bloomberg shows fluoride-free sales far outpacing fluoridated paste in 2025. Political momentum is a major driver: Utah and Florida banned water fluoridation in 2025, RFK Jr./HHS has acted against fluoride, the FDA has restricted ingestible fluoride for children, and states have opened probes into fluoride toothpaste marketing. Toothpaste fluoride itself has not been federally restricted.

### Cited Findings
- Per NielsenIQ data reported by Bloomberg, sales of fluoride-free toothpaste grew about 16% on average each month through April 2025, versus +2.4% for fluoridated toothpaste. Analysts link this partly to RFK Jr.'s commentary, although Kennedy has said he supports fluoride in toothpaste. — [Gulf News (Bloomberg)](https://gulfnews.com/world/americas/does-your-toothpaste-have-fluoride-rfk-jr-spurs-fluoride-free-movement-1.500167982)
- In 2025, 15% of US toothpaste users said they considered fluoride-free options when buying toothpaste. — [Omya](https://www.omya.com/en/omya-specialty-materials/industries/personal-care-and-home-care/personal-care-and-home-care-articles/the-rise-of-fluoride-free-oral-care)
- Mintel's "Future of Oral Care 2025" argues that consumers who reject fluoride "aren't rejecting oral care; they're seeking alternatives they feel good about," and flags natural fluoride-free and microbiome-gentle opportunities. Mintel's US Oral Care 2026 report says whitening, electric add-ons and natural toothpaste "remain resilient." — [Mintel Future of Oral Care 2025](https://store.mintel.com/report/the-future-of-oral-care); [Mintel US Oral Care 2026](https://store.mintel.com/report/us-oral-care-market-report)
- Fluoride-free toothpaste market (global): about USD 3.6bn in 2025, with North America at about 31.8% of revenue. Forecast: USD 3.9bn in 2026 rising to USD 8.1bn by 2033. — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/fluoride-free-toothpaste-market-report)
- Nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste (global): USD 422m in 2025, with a 19.0% CAGR to 2033. Other estimates range from USD 0.9bn to USD 1.53bn in 2025, depending on definition. — [Grand View nano-HAp](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/nano-hydroxyapatite-toothpaste-market-report); [Growth Market Reports](https://growthmarketreports.com/report/hydroxyapatite-toothpaste-market); [Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/hydroxyapatite-toothpaste-market)
- Boka (nano-HAp) launched a probiotic toothpaste in September 2025 and sells at both Erewhon and Walmart, spanning prestige to mass. — [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/oral-cares-second-wave-is-all-about-the-microbiome)
- Utah HB 81 (signed March 2025, effective 7 May 2025) was the first statewide ban on adding fluoride to public water. It also lets pharmacists prescribe fluoride supplements without a dental referral. — [Heartland](https://heartland.org/publications/utahs-first-state-to-ban-on-water-fluoridation/); [Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/2026/02/01/after-utah-bans-it-water-fluoride/)
- Florida became the second state to ban fluoridation (signed by DeSantis, effective 1 July 2025). Its law bars additives not aimed at meeting drinking-water safety standards and does not name fluoride. — [Yahoo/AP](https://www.yahoo.com/news/florida-becomes-2nd-state-ban-202508149.html); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/fluoride-ban-states-cavities-kids-florida-utah-rcna209750)
- HHS Secretary RFK Jr. said he would direct the CDC to stop recommending community water fluoridation and form a task force. Similar state bills (for example Arizona and South Carolina) were under consideration in 2026. CareQuest maintains a 2026 state policy map, dated 25 Sep 2026, that I could not open. — [CareQuest 2026 map](https://carequest.org/resource/2026-community-water-fluoridation-policy-state-legislative-activity/); [WaterVerge 2026 update](https://www.waterverge.com/news/fluoride-controversy-2026/)
- FDA: moved against unapproved ingestible fluoride drugs for children. These are no longer recommended for children under 3 or for older children without high caries risk. Instead of seeking full removal from the market, the FDA sent letters to four companies. The action **does not cover toothpaste or rinses**, and the FDA recommends topical fluoride such as toothpaste as the alternative. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fda-fluoride-ingestible-products-kids-teeth-remove-market/); [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/fda-and-rfk-jr-aim-to-remove-fluoride-supplements-used-to-protect-kids-teeth); [Drug Discovery Trends](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/fda-moves-against-unapproved-ingestible-fluoride-drugs-for-kids-citing-risk-benefit-concerns/)
- In January 2026, CNN and ProPublica reported that Kennedy and fluoride skeptics have moved from water to "obstructing other fluoride sources." The Salt Lake Tribune (Feb 2026) reports that fluoride treatments are being targeted by the FDA and other states after Utah's ban. — [CNN/ProPublica](https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/16/health/fluoride-changes-propublica); [Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/2026/02/01/after-utah-bans-it-water-fluoride/)
- Texas AG Ken Paxton opened investigations into Colgate-Palmolive and P&G over how they market fluoride toothpaste to parents and children. This appeared in the search summary for the fluoride/FDA query; I did not confirm it on a specific page. — [search result set incl. Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/americas/does-your-toothpaste-have-fluoride-rfk-jr-spurs-fluoride-free-movement-1.500167982)
- Moon sells a fluoride-free whitening gel toothpaste at Ulta. — [Ulta](https://www.ulta.com/p/stain-removal-fluoride-free-gel-whitening-toothpaste-pimprod2022567?sku=2576020)

### Inferences
- In the US, "fluoride-optional" positioning (nano-HAp as the enamel-repair hero) is now mainstream demand, not fringe. The format and claims strategy needs a clear stance: offer both fluoride and nano-HAp variants, or a combined formula. Dental bodies (ADA, CDC historically) strongly favour fluoride, and less water fluoridation raises the importance of topical fluoride for caries risk. That creates a tension: public-health need for fluoride versus consumer pull toward fluoride-free.
- Kids' toothpaste is the most politically exposed sub-segment (Texas probe, FDA supplement action). Kids' fluoride-free and HAp formats are likely to see growing parental interest in the US.
- In Europe, nano-HAp is constrained by the EU SCCS opinion on nano-hydroxyapatite (this is background knowledge, not sourced in this research; see Gaps). Fluoride politics is mainly a US phenomenon.

### Gaps
- No US retail scanner sales figures were found for named HAp brands (Boka, Davids, Risewell, Dr. Jen, Apagard).
- No European data on fluoride-free share was found. The EU regulatory status of nano-HAp in toothpaste was not verified in this session.
- No finalised CDC guidance change was confirmed, and the status as of October 2026 of the 2026 state bills (AZ, SC, others) is unconfirmed because the CareQuest map could not be opened.

## 4. Benefit trends: whitening, sensitivity, gum health, enamel, microbiome, skinification, TikTok brands, flavour

### Takeaway
Demand is converging on stacked "clinical cosmetic" benefits: whitening with sensitivity relief, gum health with hyaluronic acid, and enamel repair through HAp. Brands are borrowing skincare language (microbiome, prebiotic, probiotic, hyaluronic). Oral care now sits in beauty retail (Sephora, Ulta), and incumbents are launching Gen-Z sub-brands. TikTok-native whitening brands have scaled fast but appear to be maturing, with Hismile revenue falling in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Sensodyne Clinical White (sensitivity plus whitening) has driven share gains and +130 bps penetration, especially among younger consumers. — [Haleon FY2025](https://www.haleon.com/content/dam/haleon/corporate/documents/investors/results/full-year-results-2025/fy25-statement.pdf.downloadasset.pdf)
- A market report cites Haleon research saying more than 80% of sensitivity sufferers also want whiter teeth. I could not verify this in Haleon's own publications. — [Mintel search set / market report](https://store.mintel.com/report/us-oral-care-market-report)
- parodontax Gum Strengthen & Protect (with hyaluronic acid) is in 18 markets and performing strongly in the US in H1 2026. parodontax grew double-digit globally in 2025. — [Haleon H1 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001900304/000110465926088450/hln-20260630xex99d1.htm)
- Colgate Optic White Pro Series (5% H2O2) is a mass-brand push into professional-strength whitening. — [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/colgate-palmolive-company-unveils-new-optic-white-pro-series-toothpaste-ce7e5ed8dd88f122)
- "Skinification": skincare terms such as prebiotic and microbiome are being applied to toothpaste. Oral care has entered beauty departments at Sephora, Ulta and Neiman Marcus. — [Highsnobiety](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/skinification-of-oral-dental-care/); [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/beauty/oral-care-brands-are-marketing-themselves-as-beauty-products/)
- BeautyMatter describes oral care's "second wave" as being about the microbiome. Boka launched a probiotic paste in September 2025. — [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/oral-cares-second-wave-is-all-about-the-microbiome)
- Colgate launched a Gen-Z sub-brand, "CO. by Colgate," exclusively at Ulta, framed as "oral beauty." — [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/colgate-goes-after-gen-z-with-new-sub-brand--175255)
- Marvis is known for metal tubes and unusual flavours and is sold via Sephora.com, Revolve and Neiman Marcus. Moon's whitening pen sold more than USD 1m at Ulta (2019-era figure). — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/beauty/oral-care-brands-are-marketing-themselves-as-beauty-products/)
- UK trade press describes oral care as having "continued stunning growth as disruptors fuel the beautification of the category." — [Marketing Now](https://www.marketing-now.co.uk/article/187705/beauty--wellness-briefing-oral-care-continues-stunning-growth-as-disruptors-fuel-the-beautification-of-the-category)
- Hismile (TikTok and influencer-led whitening): revenue reportedly fell to about USD 153m in 2025 after a USD 102m cut in marketing spend, while net profit rose from USD 9.4m to USD 27.5m (per The Australian, via secondary sources). Gross-sales claims of A$700m (2024) and "USD 300m sold last year" conflict. — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/opinion/20000-to-150-million-financial-teardown-hismile-viral-playbook/); [SBO Financial](https://sbo.financial/blog/financial-teardowns/hismile-business-strategy/); [Bond University](https://research.bond.edu.au/en/publications/hismile-bootstrapping-an-oral-care-industry-disruptor/); [UQ Ventures](https://ventures.uq.edu.au/blog/2025/09/inside-hismile-20k-startup-global-oral-care-dynamo)
- TikTok Shop: a whitening mouthwash sold 91,900 units in 30 days, worth about USD 1.81m in US TikTok Shop sales. This shows that oral care sells through social commerce. — [tktk.com](https://www.tktk.com/en/jiqiao/3698.html)

### Inferences
- The winning benefit stack for 2026–2030 looks like whitening + sensitivity + enamel repair, plus gum/microbiome care, delivered with beauty-grade sensorials and packaging. A new format could naturally "dose" stacked or customised benefits, for example separate AM/PM or whitening/repair units.
- Hismile's 2025 trajectory (revenue down, profit up as paid social was cut) suggests TikTok-led acquisition is expensive and volatile. Social virality is a launch accelerator, not a durable moat.
- Flavour and sensorial differentiation (Marvis) earns beauty-retail placement and premium prices.

### Gaps
- No quantified sales data for oral microbiome or probiotic toothpaste, breath-specific lines, or flavour-led brands (Marvis, Twice) in 2024–2026.
- No sizing of the Sephora/Ulta oral care sub-category.

## 5. Segments: travel, kids, elderly/dry mouth, men, Gen Z

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. The only quantified segment signals are Gen Z/younger consumers, who respond to premium clinical-cosmetic launches (Sensodyne Clinical White) and beauty-retail sub-brands (Colgate CO. at Ulta), and travel as a cited tablet use case.

### Cited Findings
- Younger consumers drove the +130 bps penetration gain from Sensodyne Clinical White. — [Haleon FY2025](https://www.haleon.com/content/dam/haleon/corporate/documents/investors/results/full-year-results-2025/fy25-statement.pdf.downloadasset.pdf)
- Colgate's Gen-Z "CO." sub-brand launched exclusively at Ulta. — [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/colgate-goes-after-gen-z-with-new-sub-brand--175255)
- Travellers are a core niche for tablets because the solid format avoids leaks and liquid limits. — [Shark Tank Success (Bite)](https://sharktanksuccess.com/bite-toothpaste-update-after-shark-tank/)
- Kids: there is FDA and state-level pressure on fluoride for children, and NBC models that kids' cavities would rise by millions if every state banned fluoridation. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/fluoride-ban-states-cavities-kids-florida-utah-rcna209750); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fda-fluoride-ingestible-products-kids-teeth-remove-market/)

### Inferences
- Gen Z is the clearest premium-growth cohort and buys through beauty retail and social commerce.
- Kids' toothpaste in the US is being reshaped by parental fluoride anxiety. This is an opening for pre-dosed, low-ingestion-risk formats that address the "how much paste" problem for under-6s.

### Gaps
- No data was found on elderly/dry-mouth demand, men's grooming oral care, or the size of the travel-size segment. These need dedicated searches (Biotène/Haleon dry-mouth sales, ageing demographics).

## 6. Channels and price points

### Takeaway
Mass grocery still dominates US volume (about 41% via supermarkets and hypermarkets). Growth channels are Amazon, DTC subscription (Bite, Hismile), TikTok Shop, beauty specialty (Sephora, Ulta) and, in Germany, the drugstore duopoly of dm and Rossmann. Price ladders run from about USD 3–5 for mass paste to USD 8–11 for premium mass (Optic White Pro) and higher for prestige beauty brands.

### Cited Findings
- Supermarkets and hypermarkets hold 41% of US toothpaste sales (2025). — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/united-states-toothpaste-market)
- Optic White Pro Series is reportedly about USD 8–11 per tube in the US (low-reliability source). — [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/colgate-optic-white-pro-series-toothpaste-colgate-bets-on-stronger/69691602)
- Bite sells through a DTC subscription and Amazon. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/06/03/how-a-founder-turned-6000-into-a-multimillion-dollar-toothpaste-business.html)

## 7. Sensory and convenience pain points with tube paste

### Takeaway
I found no direct consumer-survey quantification of tube pain points such as mess, over-dosing, sharing hygiene or squeezing out the last of the paste. The evidence is indirect: tablet and bit brands market leak-free, pre-dosed, travel-friendly and subscription convenience, while their own barriers (texture, chewing, taste) show that switching formats has a sensory cost.

### Cited Findings
- Tablet benefits are cited as leak-free travel and freshness through subscription. — [Shark Tank Success](https://sharktanksuccess.com/bite-toothpaste-update-after-shark-tank/)
- Tablet barriers: chewing before brushing feels unfamiliar, and texture, taste and price limit adoption. — [IJSRT](https://www.ijsrtjournal.com/article/a-comprehensive-review-on-toothpaste-tablets-formulation-benefits-and-future-prospects); [Future Market Insights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/toothpaste-tablets-market)

### Inferences
- An innovative format has to keep paste-like foam and mouthfeel while solving dosing, mess and waste. The tablet experience shows consumers will not trade sensory familiarity for sustainability alone.

### Gaps
- No quantitative consumer data on tube-related frustrations (mess, dosing, sharing, residual waste). This would need primary research or paywalled Mintel or Kantar reports.
