# Private-label / contract sun care (SPF) manufacturers: UK, Ireland, Nordics, Baltics, Poland, CZ/SK, HU, SI, HR, Greece, Turkey (non-EU)

> **Method and reliability note (read first).** These notes come from about 25 web searches in October 2026. Two limits cut the session short. First, the egress proxy blocked **every** company domain I tried to open directly: przemyslkosmetyczny.pl, equalanpharma.eu, cleansery.com, simelia.gr, propharm.com.gr, aerosol.pl, oemhallmark.com, cosmeticindex.com, cosmiko.co.uk, privatelabelscosmetics.com, dr-miele.eu and selfnamed.com. Second, the shared web-search budget ran out (200 searches per turn across all agents) before I had covered Ireland, Denmark, Finland, Norway beyond one lead, Estonia, Lithuania, Slovakia, Hungary, Slovenia and Croatia. That also left most of the Polish brand-owner candidates unchecked.
>
> So every claim below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from full pages I read myself. Supplier details (MOQs, formats, SPF claims) are mostly **self-reported marketing copy** or **listicles written by competing Chinese manufacturers** (xiran*, desifine, rixin), and the report writer should treat them as leads, not verified facts. Where I could not find data, it says "not published — ask". I invented no MOQs or prices.

## Q1. Which companies in these countries make sun care (SPF) for third-party brands, and is each real and active in sun care?

### Takeaway
Sun care was **directly evidenced** (an SPF product or "sunscreen" in a private-label or contract offer) for only a handful of companies in scope:
- UK: Cosmiko and Group55.
- Poland: Clover Cosmetics, Equalan Pharma, Cleansery and Aii Sp. z o.o. (plus an unclear CosLab mention).
- Greece: Simelia, Propharm and MADIS.
- Turkey: Elya, Bulut, Alin Global, IGN, SCK Zeta, Humay, Sheamona and ELAN.

Several of the most relevant names exist and do private label or aerosols, but I found **no source tying them to sun care**: kdc/one Swallowfield, Dr. Miele Cosmed (ex-Global Cosmed), Aerosol Service, Paragon Nordic, Selfnamed and Evy Technology.

I found nothing at all for Ireland, Denmark, Finland, Estonia, Lithuania, Slovakia, Hungary, Slovenia or Croatia (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- **For a micro-MOQ start in scope, Cosmiko (UK, 250 units) is the only sourced SPF option below 1,000 units.** It sells creams in airless bottles, not sprays, mousses or sun waters. Clover (PL, 1,000 pcs / 25 kg) and Elya (TR, 1,000 units) are the next tier.
- Poland's large private-label sector (Dr. Miele Cosmed, PCC Kosmet, Delia, Marcon-Avista) appears built around **retailer private label at retail-chain volumes**. A D2C start-up will probably meet much higher MOQs there; the EEN profile's 2,500–10,000 benchmark points the same way. This is an inference, not a published MOQ.
- Polish brands sell SPF 50 face mists, including an aerosol "invisible" mist (AVA). So Polish fillers with UV-filter spray experience probably exist, but the source documents do not identify the contract maker.

- **EU testing context** that each supplier's dossier should meet:
  - SPF is normally tested in vivo to ISO 24444. Two new ISO alternative SPF methods have been available since 2025, and products already tested to 24444 need not be re-tested. Sources: [Intertek – EU sunscreen regulations (Aug 2025)](https://www.intertek.com/blog/2025/08-04-eu-regulations-for-sunscreen-products/); [GCI / CTPA update](https://www.gcimagazine.com/home/article/22945667/ctpa-eu-regulatory-update-a-reminder-on-rules-for-sunscreen-standards-plus-two-new-tests-for-spf)
  - UVA-PF must be at least ⅓ of the labelled SPF, and the circled "UVA" logo needs a critical wavelength of at least 370 nm.
  - "Water resistant" claims need test support.
  - "Sunblock" and "total protection" claims are banned under Recommendation 2006/647/EC, which is under revision.
  - CPNP notification and a PIF are needed before sale. Source for these four points: [Intertek](https://www.intertek.com/blog/2025/08-04-eu-regulations-for-sunscreen-products/)
- **Enforcement:** the EU's 2025 JACOP market-surveillance project focused on sun protection products, judged against Recommendation 2006/647/EC. Source: [JACOP 2025 report (PDF)](https://www.pap.pl/sites/default/files/2026-07/JACOP%202025%20-%20REPORT.pdf)
- **Importer as Responsible Person:** for Turkish (non-EU) supply, the EU importer is the Responsible Person by default unless the brand designates another. Source: [Intertek](https://www.intertek.com/blog/2025/08-04-eu-regulations-for-sunscreen-products/). The search extract attributed this to EU guidance in the Turkey results.

### Inferences
- Because no supplier publishes its stock-formula SPF dossiers, the client should make **"send the ISO 24444 + ISO 24443 report (and ISO 16217/18861 water-resistance report if claimed) for the exact formula and pack"** a screening question. Stock formulas are only "minimum fixed cost" if that test data comes with them. Re-testing a new formula typically costs a meaningful fixed amount, but no figure was sourced in this session.
- Cosmiko and Clover are the closest in-scope fit to "stock SPF formula, low MOQ". Neither offers a mousse or sun-water format in the sources found.
- Cleansery's split of 6–10 weeks (stock) vs 3–6 months (custom) is a useful planning benchmark for the stock-versus-custom decision.

## Q3. Which can fill aerosol / bag-on-valve / foamer for an SPF mousse, and which can make a water-thin "sun water" / transparent SPF spray (Lancaster Sun Beauty Sun Protective Water SPF 50-type)?

### Takeaway
**No in-scope supplier publicly offers a stock SPF mousse or a stock sun-water formula.** For a mousse, the realistic route is an aerosol filler that already makes cosmetic mousses or bag-on-valve packs, paired with a custom or licensed SPF formula: kdc/one Swallowfield (UK), Paragon Nordic / Unaro (SE/NO), Aerosol Service (PL), and possibly WATS (PL). Evy Technology owns a patented SPF mousse but shows no OEM programme. For a sun water, nothing in scope was found. The Polish SPF-mist brands (AVA, AA, Paese) and the Turkish fason makers are the nearest leads to probe.

### Inferences
- **Mousse:** an SPF mousse needs (a) a foamable SPF emulsion, (b) aerosol or bag-on-valve filling, and (c) SPF testing on the finished foam format. A pack change can invalidate existing SPF data, a point one source makes about format and packaging changes generally. Of the in-scope fillers, Aerosol Service (PL) and kdc/one Swallowfield (UK) document both mousse/BOV capability and private-label or contract service. Paragon Nordic is the only one where a source, albeit a weak one, links aerosols to sun care. All three will likely have aerosol-scale MOQs, which are not published. A **non-aerosol pump foamer** (e.g. an Airspray-type mechanical foamer) could cut the filler requirement, but no in-scope supplier was found advertising an SPF formula validated in a mechanical foamer.
- **Sun water:** Lancaster-type sun waters are typically bi-phase or high-ethanol transparent fluids, as the Caudalie and SVR examples show. A low-MOQ cream lab (Cosmiko, Clover) is unlikely to have one in stock. Suppliers that already make SPF "mists" or transparent sprays are the right targets, but none in scope was confirmed. The best leads to probe are the makers behind the Polish AVA, Paese and AA mists, plus the Turkish fason SPF makers (Elya, SCK Zeta).
