# Private-label / contract sun care manufacturers in DE, AT, CH, NL, BE (low-MOQ focus, SPF foam and sun water)

> **Method note for the report writer.** Every company website fetch (WebFetch) was blocked by this environment's egress proxy (cosmetize.nl, systemkosmetik.de, riviera.at, mann-schroeder.de, labtree.de, dermcos.de and aerosol-service.com were all refused). Partway through, the session's shared web-search budget also ran out. All findings below therefore come from search-engine result extracts. These quote the pages listed, but I could not open the pages myself. Treat every MOQ and price as "quoted on the vendor/directory page as indexed, re-confirm before relying on it". Most directory entries (wlw, Europages) are self-descriptions written by the companies. Candidates I could not check are listed under Gaps rather than guessed at.

## 1. Which companies in DE/AT/CH/NL/BE actually make sun care for third-party brands?

### Takeaway
The region has about 25 credible contract or private-label makers. Only about a dozen show documented sun care. The truly low-MOQ, stock-formula SPF options are small: Cosmacon/Tojo Cosmetics (Hamburg, from 25–100 units), Private Skin Lab (Salzburg, from 50 units), KHK (sticks only) and Cosmetize (NL, from about 1,000–2,500 units). The big sun specialists (Mann & Schröder, Emil Kiessling, Win Cosmetic/Dalli, Cosmetic Service, Steinfels Swiss) serve retailer house brands and publish no MOQ. SystemKosmetik's "~3,000 MOQ" could not be verified, and SBLC has no own production and no sun care listed.

## 3. Who can make (b) a watery "sun water", transparent or hydroalcoholic SPF spray like Lancaster Sun Protective Water SPF 50?

### Takeaway
No DACH/Benelux supplier was found that explicitly advertises a hydroalcoholic or "sun water" SPF base for private label. The closest leads are:
- Dermcos: holds a patent on a transparent high-UVA sunscreen.
- artefactum: lists "Solar Facial Invisible Protection" in its private-label sun range.
- Cosmetize: stock SPF sprays, though they are "100% natural/mineral", which is the opposite of a clear water.
- The big sun specialists (Kiessling, Mann & Schröder, Win Cosmetic), which make transparent sprays for retailers. That last point is inferred and unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Dermcos GmbH: patent "Transparent sunscreen with high UVA protection"; its revenue comes mainly from sunscreens — [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/Dermcos%20GmbH,%20Walldorf/Amtsgericht%20Mannheim%20HRB%20745251)
- artefactum: private-label sun category includes "Solar Facial Invisible Protection" — [Europages artefactum](https://www.europages.de/ARTEFACTUM-GMBH/00000005311420-001.html)
- Cosmetize: spray SKUs from 2,500 × 200 ml, vegan, 100% natural; the stock range is pitched as "100% natural and mineral" — [Cosmetize sprays](https://cosmetize.nl/product/suncare/sprays/); [Cosmetize suncare](https://cosmetize.nl/producten/suncare/)
- Category benchmark: Eucerin Sun Spray Transparent Sensitive Protect SPF 50 shows that clear SPF 50 body sprays are an established mass format — [Eucerin](https://int.eucerin.com/products/sun-protection/sun-spray-transparent-sensitive-protect-spf-50)
- Search for EU hydroalcoholic private-label sun sprays returned no DACH/Benelux supplier. The nearest EU option with a published MOQ was Valquer (Spain, from 1,000 units, acts as Responsible Person), which is out of scope for this note — [Valquer sunscreen](https://info.valquer.com/en/cosmetic-manufacturer/sunscreen/)

### Inferences
- A Lancaster-style "protective water" is technically a very thin, high-water, often low-alcohol emulsion or bi-phase. Hydroalcoholic variants are flammable, which raises filling-room (ATEX) and shipping (dangerous goods) issues. Suppliers with solvent and aerosol infrastructure (ASM, the Dalli/Win group, the IGA aerosol fillers) are the likeliest to handle alcohol-rich bulk. This is general knowledge, not sourced.
- Natural or mineral stock sprays (Cosmetize) generally cannot be transparent. A clear "sun water" therefore probably needs organic-filter custom development, so expect a development fee plus about €1,500–5,000 or more, plus the full SPF test cost, not white-label pricing. The development-fee range comes from [meine-eigenmarke costs](https://www.meine-eigenmarke.de/kosten-ablaeufe-eigenmarke/); the rest is inference.

### Gaps
- No supplier confirmed hydroalcoholic capability, transparent stock SPF spray formulas, or water-thin SPF 50 bases with ISO 24444/24443 data.
- Lancaster's own manufacturer and that product's formulation details were not researched (out of scope / no searches left).
- Price-per-unit signals for transparent SPF sprays from DACH/Benelux makers: none found.
