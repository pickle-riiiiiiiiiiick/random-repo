# Italian private-label / contract (terzista) sun care manufacturers — SPF foam/mousse and "sun water" focus, low MOQ

Research notes, October 2026. Method: WebSearch only. Direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) to every supplier domain tried were blocked by the network egress proxy, so every fact below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of each page. Several supplier pages are flagged by the search index as 1–4 years old; where that matters it is noted. The shared web-search budget ran out before the last verification round, so some candidates on the brief's list are listed under Gaps as "not researched". Anything without a published figure is marked "not published — ask".

---

## Q1. Which Italian companies really offer private-label / conto-terzi sunscreens? (verification of the candidate list + new finds)

### Takeaway
About 20 Italian firms have verifiable evidence of making SPF products for other brands. They fall into three groups: (1) micro-MOQ white-label catalogue labs (3–25 units, fixed stock formulas, change only the label), (2) small and medium terzisti with stock sun ranges and custom development (about 200 units and up per SKU), and (3) industrial aerosol and bag-on-valve (BoV) plants that can make mousse and sprays but publish no MOQ. Most of the big names on the brief's candidate list are colour-cosmetics or fragrance specialists with no public evidence of sun care contract work in Italy. Those are Intercos, Ancorotti, Chromavis, Gotha, Italcos, ICR and Kemon.

### Inferences
- **Mousse shortlist:** (1) Biofarma San Pietro Viminario, the only explicit sun-mousse capability, likely with industrial MOQs. (2) Everton, which has BoV sun experience and aerosol mousse lines; ask whether it can run a foaming SPF emulsion. (3) Cosmosol, high-capacity BoV, sun not confirmed.
- **Low-MOQ mousse route:** none of the micro-MOQ labs shows aerosol capability. A practical route is a non-aerosol pump-foamer bottle filled by a mid-tier lab such as Sabatel, Macosgroup, Licofarma or Jiabi Lab. No Italian lab explicitly advertises this for SPF, so the foam base would need custom development plus its own ISO 24444 testing, because foam application thickness affects SPF.
- **Sun water:** Sabatel and Zetaphar are the most direct stock-formula starting points. Sabatel's own 2026 note suggests its transparent range tops out at SPF 30, so an SPF 50 Lancaster-type water may need custom work. Bios Line has demonstrably made an alcohol-based SPF 50 transparent spray, but it is unconfirmed whether it will make one for others.
- **Hydroalcoholic handling (inference):** flammable alcohol-based bulk needs ATEX-rated handling. That favours aerosol and fragrance-capable plants: Everton, Biofarma San Pietro Viminario, Cosmosol and ICR. ICR is a fragrance house with no SPF evidence; it might fill alcohol bases but would not hold the SPF formula.
- **Market difference:** the FDA mousse warning matters only if the client sells in the US. The EU Cosmetics Regulation has no equivalent dosage-form restriction (general knowledge, not verified in this research). Child-appeal packaging is still worth avoiding.

### Gaps
- No Italian supplier published an MOQ, price or lead time for aerosol, BoV or mousse SPF products. Everton, Biofarma and Cosmosol all need direct RFQs.
- No source confirmed any Italian supplier holds a ready, ISO 24444-tested SPF 50 hydroalcoholic "sun water" for white label. Sabatel's SPF 50 transparent spray is listed in its catalogue, but its 2026 news contradicts it.
- The vehicle (alcohol, water or silicone) of Zetaphar's "acqua solare spray", Sabatel's "transparent" sprays and Laboratori Italiani's "sun tanning water" is not stated. Ask for INCI lists.
- Non-aerosol foamer capability (Airspray-type pumps) for SPF was not found at any Italian supplier. Unknown, not ruled out.
- Kompass-listed aerosol fillers (F.I.A., Volcke Aerosol Italy, Areco Italia) were not checked.
