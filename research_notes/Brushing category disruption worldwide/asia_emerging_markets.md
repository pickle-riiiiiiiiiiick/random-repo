## 1. China: size, local vs foreign shift, e-commerce/livestream, premium functional paste, electric-brush boom and bust

### Takeaway
China's toothpaste market is now led by domestic brands. Yunnan Baiyao has held about 25% for years. The 2025 retail top five are Yunnan Baiyao, Haolai/Darlie (好来), the Douyin-native Canban (参半), Saky (舒客) and Lengsuanling (冷酸灵/Dencare). Crest and Colgate have dropped out of the top five. Disruption runs through Douyin livestream/content commerce: Douyin is the No.1 online toothpaste channel, and Douyin plus Tmall hold about two-thirds of online sales. ByteDance-backed Canban grew 82.5% in 2025 to RMB 2.5bn and filed for a Hong Kong IPO in March 2026. Electric toothbrushes boomed until about 2021 and then contracted. Online e-brush sales fell 6.9% in 2024, Philips reported double-digit China declines, and domestic Usmile overtook Philips online but stalled (regulatory fine, reported failed IPO in 2025).

- The electric toothbrush category looks like it peaked around 2021–2023 by value. A declining market plus price competition hurt both Philips and the domestic leader Usmile.

## 2. Japan: Lion, Kao, Sunstar, Sangi; liquid/foam/enzyme; 1450 ppm fluoride; elderly care; interdental culture

### Takeaway
Japan is a mature, Lion-led market. Euromonitor (via search excerpt) puts Lion at about 29% of oral care value in 2025, up from 25% in 2020, against about 15% for Sunstar. Innovation is about premium actives and formats: enzyme pastes (Lion Clinica PRO), high-concentration TXA/IPMP gum care, Kao's liquid toothpaste, nano-hydroxyapatite (Sangi Apagard), and high-fluoride (1,450 ppm) pastes. The 1,450 ppm pastes followed Japan's approval of a 1,500 ppm upper limit, with MHLW labelling rules issued on 17 March 2017. Ageing drives root-caries, gum-care, denture and dry-mouth products.

### Cited Findings
- **Shares:** Lion held about 29% of oral care value in 2025 (25% in 2020), with Sunstar about 15% (Euromonitor, via search excerpt). — [Euromonitor Oral Care in Japan](https://www.euromonitor.com/oral-care-in-japan/report)
- Lion claims No.1 share in toothpaste and toothbrushes in Japan (2024 company research). — [Lion Business Outline 2026](https://www.lion.co.jp/en/ir/pdf/outline/business-outline_2026en.pdf)
- Lion's domestic oral care sales were about JPY 80.2bn in FY2025, up 4.7% (third-party aggregator, unverified against the filing). Lion's Q2 2025 Q&A cited a temporary demand drop after 2024 launches and a plan to push high-end toothpaste in H2. — [Envalith (aggregator)](https://envalith.com/en/companies/4912/segments); [Lion Q2 2025 Q&A](https://www.lion.co.jp/en/ir/pdf/fs/2025/25_2q_as.pdf)
- Lion's overseas sales to external customers were JPY 158bn in 2025, with oral care a top-priority category overseas. — [Lion Global Strategy 2026](https://www.lion.co.jp/en/ir/pdf/presentations/global_strategy2026en.pdf)
- **Enzyme paste:** Lion's CLINICA PRO periodontal barrier paste launched nationwide in January 2025 as "the only toothpaste in Japan formulated with an enzyme as an active ingredient". Lion also launched a September 2025 gum-care product with its highest-ever concentration of TXA (anti-inflammatory) and IPMP (antibacterial). — [Towards Healthcare (vendor) – Japan oral care companies](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/companies/japan-oral-care-companies)
- **Fluoride 1,450 ppm:**
  - Japan approved dentifrices with a 1,500 ppm upper limit for the first time (Japanese Society for Oral Health statement).
  - MHLW issued a notice on 17 March 2017 (薬生薬審発0317第1号) on precautions for fluoride pastes above 1,000 ppm. The industry body set a voluntary labelling standard the same day (日歯工第19号), revised in August 2020.
  - Many mainstream Japanese pastes now carry 1,450 ppm, e.g. Lion Clinica Advantage, NONIO, Systema and Check-Up rootcare α, and Sunstar Ora2. Sources disagree on several SKUs (Kao Clear Clean, GUM, Check-Up standard are listed at 950 vs 1,450 ppm).
  - Sources: [MHLW notice PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11120000/000687395.pdf); [JSOH statement 2018](https://www.dent-kng.or.jp/fswp/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/statement_20180301.pdf); [Metropolis Japan drugstore guide](https://metropolisjapan.com/japan-drugstore-guide-fluoride-oral-care/)
- **Liquid toothpaste:** Kao Clear Clean liquid is a medicated quasi-drug, used as a 15 ml swish for 20 s and then brushing, with no rinse needed. Kao groups it under "Liquid toothpaste・Foaming toothpaste". — [Kao product page](https://www.kao.com/jp/en/products/clearclean/4901301203250/) (from earlier notes)
- **nHAp:** Sangi's Apagard has been sold since 1980 and is now sold at US retail. — [Today's RDH](https://www.todaysrdh.com/?p=10226); [Weee! listing](https://www.sayweee.com/en/product/apagard-premio-hydroxyapatite-toothpaste/3000816) (from earlier notes)
- **Ageing:** A vendor report estimates the geriatric segment at about 27% of Japanese oral care in 2025 (adults 58%, children 15%). It says companies are developing gentle products, denture care and dry-mouth treatments, and that denture cleansers, adhesives and gum-care paste are favoured in elderly-heavy prefectures such as Shizuoka. Low-quality vendor. — [Towards Healthcare Japan oral care](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/press-release/japan-oral-care-market)
- **Market size:** estimates conflict widely, from USD 1.47bn (2024) to USD 3.98bn (2025). IMARC gives a 1.2% toothpaste CAGR. All are vendor figures. — [Towards Healthcare](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/insights/japan-oral-care-market-sizing); [Spherical Insights](https://www.sphericalinsights.com/reports/japan-oral-care-products-market); [IMARC Japan](https://imarcgroup.com/japan-toothpaste-market)

### Inferences
- Japanese value growth runs through premium medicated ("quasi-drug") claims: enzyme, TXA/IPMP and 1,450 ppm fluoride. It does not come from volume, consistent with an ageing, shrinking population.
- High-fluoride gel pastes for root caries (e.g. Check-Up rootcare α) are an elderly-specific product type with little Western mass-market equivalent.

### Gaps
- No primary data on liquid or foam toothpaste share in Japan (INTAGE/Kao IR not reachable).
- No Kao or Sunstar oral care sales figures, and no data on interdental (floss/interdental brush) usage rates.
- The exact year Japan approved the 1,500 ppm upper limit was not confirmed. The excerpts show the 17 March 2017 MHLW labelling notice, which is consistent with approval around 2017.

## 4. India: Colgate, HUL, Dabur, Patanjali; rural penetration and frequency; powders; sachets; Ayurveda; e-com/quick commerce

### Takeaway
Penetration is near-universal, but frequency and per-capita use are low. Per Kantar via Colgate (Aug 2026), 45% of rural Indians still don't brush daily (down from 55%), and 76% of urban Indians don't brush twice a day (down from 80%). Colgate's share has eroded from about 57% (FY15) to about 48% (2020) and about 42.6–43% (2024/25). Naturals/Ayurveda players took the share: Dabur at about 14–17% and Patanjali at about 11%. HUL is flat to low single-digit growth. Toothpaste is still 80% traditional trade. E-commerce is 6%, with quick commerce about 40% of that. Premium mix is rising (14.8% to 18.6%).

## 6. Middle East: GCC and Turkey; miswak pastes, halal, premium whitening; Turkish local brands

GCC toothpaste is a multinational market: Colgate, Signal (Unilever), Sensodyne (Haleon) and Oral-B (P&G). Saudi Arabia is small, at about USD 194m (2024, vendor). Latin America is Colgate's stronghold. Undated and unsourced figures put Colgate at about 77–80%+ in Mexico and about 70% of Brazilian oral care (2015). Colgate also owns Sorriso, the value brand created after Brazil's antitrust suspension of Kolynos. Latin America is Colgate's largest regional oral care profit pool: USD 4.78bn in total 2025 regional sales, flat in USD, with organic growth of 3.9%. Colgate reported toothpaste share declines in Latin America in 2025. Brazilian per-capita toothpaste consumption is about 3x India's.

## 8. Africa: Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa; habits, chewing sticks, multinationals, affordability

### Takeaway
Africa's toothpaste markets are led by multinationals: Colgate in Kenya and South Africa, Unilever's Close-Up and Pepsodent plus Colgate and Oral-B in Nigeria. Affordability shapes behaviour. Nigeria's 2024 cost-of-living crisis pushed some consumers back to chewing sticks, and to rationing paste and delaying brush replacement. Brands switch share on price (Oral-B gained, then lost share after a price rise). Hard share data is scarce and mostly old or from scan panels.

### Cited Findings
- **Nigeria:**
  - Close-Up is the most-scanned toothpaste brand (Sagaci consumer scanning panel). — [Sagaci Research Nigeria](https://sagaciresearch.com/beauty-and-personal-care-toothpaste-nigeria/)
  - Consumers moved to Oral-B and then back to Close-Up after Oral-B raised prices. They buy on budget, and Pepsodent is chosen for whitening and affordability (retailer anecdotes). — [Marketing Edge](https://marketingedge.com.ng/battle-of-toothpaste-brands-in-nigerian-market/); [Marketing Edge 2](https://marketingedge.com.ng/leading-brands-battle-for-controlling-share-of-nigerias-oral-care-market/)
  - In October 2024, the "high cost of toothpaste" pushed Nigerians back to chewing sticks. One consumer cited spending ₦4,000 on toothpaste in three weeks. — [Vanguard, Oct 2024](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2024/10/high-cost-of-toothpaste-nigerians-return-to-chewing-stick/)
  - Households ration toothpaste, delay brush replacement or use chewing sticks. The market is forecast at a 7.68% CAGR for 2026–31 (vendor). — [StrategyHelix Nigeria](https://strategyh.com/report/toothpaste-market-in-nigeria/)
  - About 540 million tubes are consumed a year (single vendor page, low quality). — [Webhaptic](https://www.webhaptic.com/oral-care-products-in-nigeria/)
- **Kenya:** Colgate had about 59% of toothpaste usage share (GeoPoll daily brand tracker, 2017). Close-Up had about 12% and Aquafresh about 11%, but the attribution to Kenya is unclear. — [GeoPoll 2017](https://www.geopoll.com/blog/colgage-dominates-kenya-oral-health-care-market/)
- **South Africa:**
  - Colgate is the most-scanned brand across 32 brands (Sagaci). — [Sagaci SA](https://sagaciresearch.com/beauty-and-personal-care-toothpaste-south-africa/)
  - The market was about USD 523.8m in 2024 (MRFR, vendor), and standard toothpaste was 73.19% in 2023 (GlobalData). — [MRFR](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/south-africa-toothpaste-market-46325); [GlobalData](https://www.globaldata.com/store/report/south-africa-toothpaste-market-analysis/)
  - South African halal body SANHA lists Sensodyne, Aquafresh, White Glo, Miswaak, Close Up, Colgate (CP SA), Pepsodent and Clicks My Earth Charcoal as halaal-suitable. Clicks is a retailer private label. — [SANHA on X, Nov 2022](https://x.com/SANHA_Halaal/status/1595143590547951617?lang=en)
- Colgate reported toothpaste share declines in Africa/Eurasia in 2025. — [Colgate 10-Q Q3 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/21665/000002166525000053/cl-20250930.htm)

### Inferences
- In Nigeria, "disruption" runs backward: affordability shocks push consumers to traditional chewing sticks and smaller or cheaper purchases. That matches the vendor note on rationing.

### Gaps
- No WHO country-profile brushing-frequency figures were retrieved for Nigeria, Kenya or SA.
- No local manufacturer shares (e.g. Nigerian or Kenyan local brands). No sachet or small-pack data.
